Getting Started

EssayCoach is a writing assistance tool built for ESL college students. It analyzes your writing and gives you specific feedback on clarity, tone, and naturalness — helping you sound more like yourself in academic English.

It is not an AI detector bypass tool. It won't guarantee any specific Turnitin or GPTZero result. Think of it as a digital writing tutor that helps you revise your own draft.

Coach reads your essay and gives you detailed feedback — sentence-level issues, scores, and specific suggestions. You stay in control of the revisions.

Humanize rewrites your essay to sound more natural. It changes phrasing, sentence rhythm, and transitions while preserving your original ideas. Use it when you want a revised draft to work from.

Credits are consumed per word processed. The cost depends on which feature you use:

• Quick Check — 1 credit/word
• Deep Feedback — 2 credits/word
• Full Review — 5 credits/word
• Humanize — 5 credits/word

Free plan users get 1 coaching session per day (up to 300 words) at no credit cost. Credits reset each billing cycle for subscription plans.

If you're just trying out the tool, start with Free — one session a day is enough to test it.

For regular use during a semester, Student Plus ($12/mo) is the most popular option. It gives you 60,000 credits — enough for multiple essays per week.

If you only need a burst of credits for finals or a big assignment, a Credit Pack is more cost-efficient than a monthly plan.

Using the Product

Choose the type that best matches what you're writing:

• Discussion Post — short, conversational responses for class
• Reflection Essay — personal, first-person writing about your experience
• Research Essay — formal academic writing with sources
• Personal Statement — college or scholarship applications
• Academic Paragraph — anything else, general academic writing

The type affects what kind of feedback the coach prioritizes — tone, formality, and structure expectations differ across types.

• AI-Likeness — how much your writing resembles patterns common in AI-generated text. Lower is better.
• Naturalness — how fluid and human your writing sounds. Higher is better.
• Personal Voice — how much your own perspective and examples come through. Higher is better.
• Clarity — how clear and well-structured your writing is. Higher is better.

These are estimates to guide your revision — not official AI detection scores. They won't match Turnitin or GPTZero results.

• Quick Check — highlights your top 3 most important issues. Great for a fast sanity check before submitting.

• Deep Feedback — full analysis of all key issues with explanations and suggestions. Best for most essays.

• Full Review — everything in Deep Feedback plus detailed rewrites for each suggestion. Use this when you have time to revise thoroughly.

Humanize works best as a starting point for your own revision, not as a final product you submit directly.

After getting the rewritten draft: read it carefully, make sure your original ideas are preserved, add your own voice back where it feels generic, and adjust anything that doesn't sound like you.

Always check whether your school allows AI-assisted writing before submitting. When in doubt, ask your instructor.
