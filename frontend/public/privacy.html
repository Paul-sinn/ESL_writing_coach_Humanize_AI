Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

We built EssayCoach for students who want to write better — not for data collection. Your writing is yours. Here's exactly what we do and don't do with your information.

🚫
Never sold
Your text and personal data are never sold to third parties.
🤖
Not used to train AI
Your writing is never used to train, fine-tune, or improve any AI model.
🗑️
Not retained
Submitted text is processed to return results, then discarded. We don't store your essays.
🔒
You're in control
You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

What We Collect

Account info
Email address, username, and nickname when you register. Used to identify your account and manage your subscription.
Submitted text
Text you paste into EssayCoach is sent to our servers and to OpenAI's API to generate feedback. It is processed in real time and not stored after results are returned.
Usage data
Basic request logs (timestamps, word counts, feature used) for billing, rate limiting, and abuse prevention. No essay content is included in logs.
Payment info
Handled entirely by our payment processor (Stripe). We never see or store your card number.

What We Never Do

Sell your data
Never We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information or submitted text with any third party for commercial purposes.
Train AI models
Never Your writing is not used to train, fine-tune, or evaluate any AI or machine learning model — ours or anyone else's.
Publish your text
Never Your submitted essays are never published, indexed, or made accessible to other users or the public.
Retain essays
Never We do not store the content of your essays after your session ends.

Third-Party Services

OpenAI
Text you submit is processed by OpenAI's API to generate writing feedback. OpenAI's own data usage policies apply to this processing. We use OpenAI's API under terms that prohibit using your data for model training.
Stripe
Payment processing. Stripe receives your payment details directly. We receive only a transaction confirmation. See stripe.com/privacy.

Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Age requirement
EssayCoach is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13 years of age. If we learn that we have inadvertently collected such information, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child under 13 has registered, contact us at privacy@essaycoach.app.

Your Rights

Access
You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you at any time.
Deletion
You can request deletion of your account and all associated personal data. Email us at privacy@essaycoach.app and we will process your request within 30 days.
Correction
You can update your email or account details at any time from your account settings.
Privacy questions? If you have any questions about this policy or want to exercise your rights, email us at privacy@essaycoach.app. We respond within 3 business days.
